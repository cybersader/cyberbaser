## Current truth (summary, 2026-08-10)

- The docs vision page carries the complete original dream, honest status on every capability, the zero-git maintainer requirement, the modern-wiki framing, and the linked-KB long arc.
- **Hub-and-spoke:** the hub is the owner-controlled publication, exact-change, integrity, trust, and review boundary. Reader controls, rich browser/WYSIWYG/CMS-like tools, forge editors, local Markdown tools, and agents are replaceable authoring spokes; renderers, crawlers, graph stores, caches, and search providers are replaceable output/network spokes.
- **One authority:** the owner authors the canonical Markdown directly in their preferred local tool. External spokes may keep block models or transient state, but they submit pinned-base proposals and cannot become a second source of truth or directly regenerate the file.
- **R12 scope:** whole-file source re-serialization is forbidden in Cyberbaser-mediated application; rich presentation remains allowed through a deliberate exact-proposal adapter. The shared one-file, one-splice proposal contract is built; no specific rich editor, intake lane, or generalized structural adapter is selected or built.
- **Federation by publication:** each base publishes its own authority; ordinary meta-wikis curate, map, annotate, mirror, index, disagree, and fork; no central Cyberbaser database or registry exists. The five-origin fixture is controlled local evidence, and a profile waits for demonstrated peer need.
- Mixed privacy, RBAC, and real-time collaboration are assigned to the private sibling boundary rather than folded into public federation.
- Explicit non-goals: not a comments system, not a content host, no direct remote writes, no global trust score, and no mandatory network service in the path of an ordinary link.
