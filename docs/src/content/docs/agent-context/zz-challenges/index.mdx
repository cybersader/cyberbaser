## What a challenge is

This folder holds briefs for problems too messy to state as a question. A challenge is:

- Grounded in a real observed friction or failure mode
- Worth multiple sessions of thinking
- Blocking progress on something upstream

**The difference from [Open Questions](/cyberbaser/reference/open-questions/):** open questions are precise and waiting for an answer. Challenges are the "I don't even know how to frame this yet" pile.

**Lifecycle of a challenge:**

```
  raw friction
      │
      ▼
  zz-challenges/*.mdx  ←──  "I'm stuck on X, let me write it out"
      │
      ├─► promoted to Design/ page  (when the shape of the solution is clear)
      ├─► promoted to Concepts/ page (when it turns out to be a primitive)
      ├─► demoted to Reference/open-questions (when it's crisp enough to just be a question)
      └─► closed with a banner  (verdict + deciding R-number + empirical or asserted)
```

The last branch is the one that was missing. A brief is not closed when the decision gets made somewhere else; it is closed when the banner lands on the brief. The one brief that was properly closed is the one that had a banner, and that is not a coincidence.
