## What the maintainer reported

Three things, in one sitting with the surface open at roughly a 960 pixel window:

1. the type and spacing still read as dense, and the spacing felt off;
2. the surface is a good enough start that the real implementation could begin; and
3. **Receipts**, **System**, **Evidence legend**, and **Checkpoint** are internal vocabulary rather than words a content-focused person would reach for.

The first and third were fixed in this pass. The second is a direction decision and is recorded as open below.

## Density

The dominant cause was not type size. The page used `calc(100% - 2rem)`, so at that window width the content hugged the viewport with a 16 pixel margin on each side and every column ran edge to edge.

The pass introduced a `--gutter` scale of `clamp(1.15rem, 4.5vw, 3.25rem)` applied to the page, header, and decision dock, then spent the recovered rhythm deliberately: more air between identity rows, longer line height on muted text, a taller reading measure at a slightly narrower column, and more padding inside both the reading surface and the exact-source block.

Duplicated chrome was removed rather than restyled. Two adjacent metadata rows became one. A single-change proposal no longer renders a change bar that restates its own title, which also removed a heading level from the page. Reading content now begins around 630 pixels instead of around 700 on that window, with four horizontal rules on screen instead of a dozen.

## Wording

Navigation now reads **Inbox · Decisions · How this works · What the labels mean · Questions for you**. Routes were deliberately left alone, so `#receipts`, `#system`, `#evidence`, and `#checkpoint` still resolve and every existing link, test, and screenshot keeps working.

Page copy behind those routes changed with them. The receipts page now leads with what a decision is and what it does not start, rather than with the word receipt. The system page asks where a proposal goes and where it stops. The evidence page says that every label is a limit on what may be claimed.

The internal terms remain correct and are still used in the documentation and in the fixture contract, where precision matters more than approachability. Naming stays open; these words are cheap to change.

This remains **static-design-only** evidence. No proposal contract, authority boundary, or owner-alpha file changed.

## Open

The maintainer's second point stands unanswered on purpose: whether to begin the real owner-review implementation now, or keep iterating on the static surface first. That is a direction decision, and the project gate says the comprehension checkpoint settles it.
