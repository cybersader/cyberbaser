## The owner rejected the review model in real use

During real hands-on inspection, the owner rejected the small-edit-centered publication review model. This is later human feedback, not another internal screenshot assessment. The earlier independent **ready for owner product-fit review** verdict closed the bounded presentation repairs but did not establish human acceptance. It is now superseded as the presentation direction by this later checkpoint.

The preceding failed interface checkpoint, initial **needs bounded repairs** review, repaired-source confirmation and mechanical results remain historical evidence in the [2026-10-30 record](/cyberbaser/agent-context/zz-log/2026-10-30-usable-owner-workspace/). That older log is unchanged.

## Document-first implementation is explicitly approved

The owner said, **“yeah build it that way and test with a full page”**.

The selected direction is:

- Make the whole reviewed document the primary reading surface, rather than organizing the experience around a small changed passage.
- Show change information contextually inline or in the margin, in the document's reading context.
- Keep a compact persistent toolbar outside the reading area or reserve space for it, rather than covering the document.
- Make **Publish** apply the whole reviewed version. It is not a selection of individual passages to publish.
- Keep previous-version, comparison and exact-source views secondary and optional. Do not present four equal tabs or nest the full document inside a changed-passage box.

This approves a presentation replacement, not a new editor, writer, artifact schema or source authority. Whole-version review and consent remain distinct from execution: the publisher still uses pinned evidence and bounded exact operations, preserves untouched bytes and retains the existing one-job and owner-authority contracts. No whole-file reserialization follows.

## Contract freeze and bounded implementation

The first documentation follow-up recorded approval while design and implementation were still pending. A subsequent independent bounded review confirms **ready for owner product-fit review**, with no blockers or required fixes. It is **not accepted by the owner**.

The complete candidate is the primary document. Readable changed blocks use the existing structural renderer rather than a source-envelope box. Block-margin marks, previous/next navigation and optional prior fragments provide context; the previous repository version, comparison and entire exact evidence are secondary tools. A compact persistent toolbar occupies reserved space outside the document scroller. The old query-mode allowlist still works without shared router or proposal-mode changes. Unsupported tables remain explicitly labelled exact source.

A new pure publication-only helper uses bounded line LCS to split a display comparison across a large authoritative splice. Intervening unchanged blocks remain unmarked. Original and refined operations pass the existing source/old-bytes/offset/replay gate; failed refinement falls back to the original operation, while an inconsistent original or absent base disables Publish on that page. Replay proves byte equivalence, not semantic accuracy or unique edit history. Derived fragments may begin or end mid-line and do not imply Markdown-block alignment. Display hunks never enter a stored artifact, request or application pipeline.

Broad rewrites and exceeded display budgets use explicitly coarse whole-document review. Individual marks and navigation are withdrawn completely rather than truncated at 500. The full candidate and exact evidence remain reachable. A structural-budget fallback retains the entire candidate as source. One Publish still approves the whole captured version and uses the unchanged native confirmation and request contract.

The independent reviewer separately ran **15 pure-display tests with 100 assertions, all passing**. This is an overlapping targeted selection, not an addition to the builder's **145 passed / three skips / 1,785 assertions** or the browser run's **six passed / 1,327 assertions**. The reviewer did not reproduce the rest of the builder suite or run a browser.

The reviewer reconstructed the seven original source files from the baseline, compared them to current source, confirmed the frozen source/security/application seams and 49 unrelated file fingerprints, verified all 55 final screenshot hashes and inspected eight recorded captures. The whole-page concern was judged addressed. The **65 builder measurements** directly record the reserved toolbar/scroller geometry during real top/middle/end scrolling, focused navigation and dynamic toolbar growth; the independent review inspected that evidence rather than remeasuring it. Expanded full-document screenshots remain composition evidence, not scrolling proof. No real-production viewport was measured.

Any larger production viewport is unmeasured, not an observed mitigation.
- A narrow-width margin badge is clipped at its left edge by about **5.6 pixels**; the digit and redundant border/focus indication remain legible.
- A standalone coarse deletion marker lacks a local previous-text disclosure. Its full pinned repository version and authoritative exact evidence remain accessible under **Review details**; no content is lost.
- The broad-rewrite threshold and desktop toolbar width remain owner-fit/tuning notes rather than speculative repair work.

The original failed browser attempt and zero-effect evidence remain preserved. This closeout changes status and evidence provenance only, not code. Human judgment of the replacement is still pending, and no real source, host, renderer, deployment or live-publication proof follows.
