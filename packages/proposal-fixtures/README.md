# `@cyberbaser/proposal-fixtures`

Private development and research metadata for coherent proposal-review experiments.

This package is not a proposal-v2 schema, queue contract, decision artifact, source writer, or product capability claim. Its independent `fixtureSchemaVersion: 1` envelope keeps executable v1, target-v2 research, conceptual later, blocked synthetic, and authority-model examples visibly separate.

Only `materializeExecutableV1Fixture(fixtureId)` may produce executable evidence. It calls the real `@cyberbaser/proposal` preparation, canonical serialization, parsing, digest, and application APIs. It rejects every other support level. The target-v2 helper is explicitly noncanonical research tooling and produces no proposal artifact.

Fixture evidence is limited to `synthetic-mechanical` and `static-design-only`. Automated reports may not claim maintainer comprehension, independent-human usability, live effects, offered lanes, public exposure, or OCI parity. Lane A and Lane B remain uninstalled, unoffered, unexposed, and undeployed. Static projection support is not lane representability: target-v2, conceptual, and authority-model fixtures are explicitly unrepresentable by current v1 lanes and same-host review mechanics. All mockup authority effects are false.

Promotion requires a separately reviewed contract decision, canonical parser/serializer work, authority review, focused maintainer comprehension, and the appropriate operational or human evidence. Green fixture tests do not promote research data into product semantics.

```bash
bun test --cwd packages/proposal-fixtures
```
